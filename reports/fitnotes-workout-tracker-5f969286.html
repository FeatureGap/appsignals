Positioning

FitNotes - Workout Tracker is positioned as the ultimate anti-bloat, reliable digital workbook for dedicated lifters who value a fast, ad-free, offline-first experience. While major competitors like Hevy and JEFIT push aggressive AI features, paywalls, and social bloat, FitNotes doubles down on pure utilitarian efficiency, frictionless logging, and robust local data ownership without subscriptions.
Quick wins: ship this week
  • Fix navigation breadcrumbs to reduce the press count when returning from exercise creation.
  • Add swipe-to-delete gestures for individual workout sets.
  • Include a date-picker toggle on the workout save screen to enable backdating.
  • Expand default exercise lists with commonly requested variations like chest press.
