Positioning

Splitser - WieBetaaltWat is positioned as the most reliable, traveler-friendly, and generously free core bill-splitting app on the market. Unlike competitors plagued by unstable updates, syncing failures, and aggressive account bugs, we focus on rock-solid core group trip utility, respecting user attention by eliminating spam notifications while preserving a frictionless experience for splitting expenses among friends and family.
Quick wins: ship this week

  • Raise the numeric input threshold for high-value currency formats in expense fields.
  • Add a quick toggle in settings to completely mute promotional alerts.
