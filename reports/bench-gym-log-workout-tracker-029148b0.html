Positioning

Bench Gym Log: Workout Tracker is the pragmatic fitness app for serious lifters who value data-driven progress over calorie counting. Unlike competitors that frustrate users with aggressive paywalls or clunky interfaces, we offer a generous, ad-free experience with a focus on long-term reliability and powerful, customizable workout tracking. Our commitment to a local-first approach ensures your data remains private and accessible, while our robust exercise library and AI-powered recommendations provide the tools you need to achieve your strength goals without the gimmicks.
