Positioning

Paladin: Learn History is positioned as the premier bite-sized history app that delivers engaging, cinematic storytelling for curious minds. Unlike competitors weighed down by rigid AI generation and forced interactions, Paladin respects the user's time and learning style by offering flexible pacing, transparent pricing, robust playback controls, and a diverse global curriculum without frustrating roadblocks.
Quick wins: ship this week

  • Include a clear 'Skip Quiz' button directly on the post-story screen.
  • Publish explicit pricing details on the initial app store description and early onboarding screens.
