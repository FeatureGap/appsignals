Positioning

Welmi - Calorie Counter & Diet is positioned as the streamlined, frictionless alternative to bloated and ad-heavy diet apps. While leveraging a beloved, highly convenient photo food scanning engine and a clear interface that drives genuine weight loss motivation, Welmi commits to absolute pricing transparency, reliable device integration, and user-first recipe planning without the aggressive paywalls and deceptive trial traps plaguing the market.
Quick wins: ship this week
  • Add a prominent, easy-to-find cancellation and trial status link directly inside the app settings to build trust against unexpected charges.
  • Introduce a quick disclaimer or edit toggle for AI-generated recipes to easily fix outlandish ingredient proportions.
  • Implement a clear 'Report inaccurate photo scan' button to collect training data and improve AI photo recognition quality.
