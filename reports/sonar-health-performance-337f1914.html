Positioning

Sonar - Health & Performance positions itself as the dependable, high-performance alternative in a market crowded by bloated applications and fragile hardware ecosystems. While competitors like Oura struggle with battery drain, sluggish performance, and strict subscription paywalls, and apps like Elite HRV suffer from outdated release cycles and connection drops, Sonar delivers a clean, lightning-fast workout logging and recovery experience. By combining rock-solid peripheral reliability with deeply actionable sleep and recovery insights, Sonar empowers athletes and health-conscious users to track performance seamlessly without software friction.
