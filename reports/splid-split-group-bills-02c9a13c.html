Positioning

Splid positions itself as the clean, lightning-fast, and ad-free alternative to bloated and aggressively monetized apps like Splitwise. By preserving an account-free, offline-first core experience while introducing critical reliability fixes and highly requested tools like manual exchange rates and receipt attachments, Splid delivers effortless group expense tracking without the heavy paywalls and friction plaguing the market.
Quick wins: ship this week
