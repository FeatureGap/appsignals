Positioning

Meal Planner is the calm, honest meal planner: drag-and-drop weekly planning that the whole household can share for free. No paywalled lists and no unreliable price gimmicks, just a plan your family will actually follow.
Quick wins: ship this week
  • Ship a free-vs-pro pricing matrix in-app and on the store listing (defuse paywall rage).
  • Fix the grocery-list crash (12 crash reports this window) - hotfix this week.
  • Add 'Add all to list' button on the week view - smallest step toward auto lists.
  • Stop promo screens for paying users within 24h; email affected users an apology credit.
