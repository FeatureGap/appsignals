Positioning

Splid - Split group bills is uniquely positioned as the frictionless, ad-free, and privacy-respecting alternative to bloated financial platforms like Splitwise. While competitors frustrate users with aggressive daily entry limits, intrusive ads, and expensive forced subscriptions, Splid wins hearts through its clean UI, reliable offline functionality, and effortless group travel expense tracking. By introducing robust multi-device syncing, receipt attachments, and manual exchange rate adjustments without compromising our lightweight ethos, we solidify our standing as the absolute best companion for group adventures and shared living expenses.
Quick wins: ship this week
  • Fix calculation and settle-up rounding errors to maintain absolute trust in financial summaries.
  • Resolve the In-App Purchase (IAP) restoration glitch triggered by Wi-Fi toggling.
  • Add support for negative input numbers to handle refunds and deposits smoothly.
  • Introduce group archiving to clean up old trip views.
