Positioning

Bench Gym Log: Workout Tracker is the privacy-first, open-source workout tracker that prioritizes user control with local-first data storage, a minimalist UI, and no aggressive paywalls. Unlike competitors, it combines the flexibility of custom routines and exercises with a focus on progressive overload tracking, all while offering a generous free version to help users build consistency without barriers.

  • Implement simple workout reminders to boost consistency
