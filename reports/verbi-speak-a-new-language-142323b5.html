Positioning

Verbi: Speak a New Language is the AI-powered language tutor that feels like talking to a native speaker, offering personalized, engaging conversations with customizable instructor personalities. Unlike competitors, Verbi combines the effectiveness of real-time speaking practice with a fun, user-friendly experience, while addressing common frustrations like slow response times and inaccurate AI understanding. With a focus on making language learning feel natural and enjoyable, Verbi is the go-to app for anyone who wants to speak a new language confidently and conversationally.
